Why people keep it

Quiet, fast, and out of the way

Most photo apps want your library in the cloud. LUMEN just wants the folders you already have.

Opens instantly

Even with tens of thousands of files. Previews are built once in the background, so the next time you open it everything is already there.

Nothing leaves your PC

No account, no cloud, no uploads, no ads. The program does not connect to the internet at all unless you ask it to open a link.

Plays your videos too

Photos open full screen with zoom and pan. Videos play inside LUMEN and pick up where you stopped last time.

Fits how you browse

Grids, tiles or a simple list. Search by name, sort by date, mark favourites, and choose how large the previews should be.

Privacy

Your photos stay yours

LUMEN has no server, no account and no analytics. It reads the folders you add and writes only its own small cache of previews, so it never touches the originals. Deleting LUMEN deletes everything it ever created.

Questions

Good to know

Do my photos get uploaded anywhere?

No. LUMEN has no cloud and no online account. Your pictures stay on your computer, and the app works the same with the internet switched off.

Can it damage or delete my files?

No. LUMEN only reads your folders. If you delete something from inside the gallery, it goes to the Windows Recycle Bin, where you can restore it as usual.

Windows warns that the publisher is unknown

The installer is not signed with a paid certificate yet, so SmartScreen asks once. Choose More info → Run anyway, or install it from the Microsoft Store once LUMEN is listed there.

Is there a version for Mac or Linux?

Not at the moment. LUMEN is made for Windows only.
